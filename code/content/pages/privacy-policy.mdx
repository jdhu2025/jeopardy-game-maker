## About this policy

This policy describes the information that may be processed when you use Quizboard Maker at [quizboardly.xyz](https://www.quizboardly.xyz). Quizboard Maker is an editable quiz-board tool for classroom review, training sessions, and group activities.

This policy is written for the current product. It does not describe features that are not currently published, including paid plans, player accounts, QR joining, or real-time cross-device multiplayer.

## Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, the service may process:

- **Topic and board content** you enter or generate, including prompts, answers, explanations, point values, audience, and language.
- **Shared-board information** when you choose to save or publish a board, such as a share identifier and the board content needed to display it.
- **Feedback information** that you voluntarily submit, such as a message or contact address.
- **Technical and usage information** such as browser type, device information, approximate timing, page path, referrer, and security or error logs.
- **Cookies or similar storage** needed for preferences, session behavior, analytics, and security. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although some product functions may not work correctly without them.

Do not enter student names, private customer information, confidential workplace information, or other sensitive data into a board unless you have a lawful reason and appropriate permission to do so.

## How we use information

We may use information to:

- provide, operate, secure, and troubleshoot Quizboard Maker;
- display, save, share, or download a board when you request those actions;
- understand product usage and improve the interface;
- respond to support requests and product feedback; and
- detect abuse, fraud, security incidents, or violations of the Terms of Service.

We do not use this policy to claim that generated quiz content is accurate, curriculum-approved, or suitable for a regulated workplace. Users are responsible for reviewing content before teaching or using it.

## Service providers and retention

We may use hosting, database, analytics, email, error-monitoring, and other infrastructure providers to operate the service. They may process information only as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, security, legal obligations, dispute handling, or legitimate business records. Retention depends on whether content is anonymous, shared, tied to an account, or included in a support request.

## Your choices and requests

You may stop using the service, clear browser storage, or contact us to ask about a privacy request. We may need enough information to identify the relevant record, and we may retain information where required for security or legal reasons.

## Children and educational use

Quizboard Maker is a tool for adults and organizations to prepare and host activities. It is not directed at collecting personal information from children. Teachers, trainers, and organizers must follow their own school, employer, and applicable privacy requirements when using the service with learners.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the product or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

For privacy questions, contact [support@quizboardly.xyz](mailto:support@quizboardly.xyz).
