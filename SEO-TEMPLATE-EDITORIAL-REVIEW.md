## How to use this record

`approved` means the generated item is internally coherent enough to carry forward to external review. It is not a subject-matter expert sign-off. `revise` means the item can remain in the starter draft after the listed edit and a domain reviewer check. `remove` means the item should not be shown in a published template. No item was marked `remove` in this pass.

External reviewer fields to complete for every item:

```text
Reviewer role:
Reviewer name or organization:
Date:
Decision: approved / revise / remove
Factual or curriculum source:
Exact edit made:
Reason for the decision:
```
